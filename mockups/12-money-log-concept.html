Rich Era — the Money Log

Lose It!'s loop, transplanted to money: a budget ring you log against, a library of reusable money items with a Money Facts label (macros = Needs / Wants / Debt / Future, micros = APR, ROI, compounding, payment plans), and bundles that log several items at once. All frames use the app's real Midnight tokens.

00

The translation

Every mechanic that makes food logging sticky has a one-to-one money equivalent. The two clever ones: % Daily Value → % of monthly budget, and exercise earning calories back → income earning budget back.

B

Money Facts — the nutrition label for a dollar

Every item in the Library carries a facts label. The right-hand column is % of your monthly budget (the % Daily Value analog). Sections appear only when they apply — a coffee has no debt block, a loan has no growth block; everything else reads n/a, just like the vitamin list. The last line is always Time cost: the hours of your life a serving costs, from your real take-home rate — the app's whole thesis in one row.

Item editor — Edit Food, for money

Everything on the Money Facts label is entered here — or filled by Autofill, which mines the item's own transaction history (and OFX imports) for typical amount, frequency, and merchant, the way Lose It's camera autofills a label. Micros are one collapsible section: skip it for a coffee, fill it for a loan.
